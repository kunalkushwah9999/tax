Account deletion

Open the web workspace, sign in and choose Account deletion in the navigation. Eligible customer accounts can remove their active application data after current-password and, when enabled, MFA verification. You can do this in a browser without reinstalling the Android app.

Owned drafts, sharing, support, feedback, credentials and related application receipts are removed together. A pseudonymous account hash, receipt hash, counts and erasure time remain for privacy and restore review. Backups, downloaded exports, external copies and browser memory are not confirmed erased.

Operator accounts and accounts with TEST payment records need reviewed ownership, provider and retention handling. Use Support → DATA_REQUEST in the workspace. Lost-password identity recovery and an external support mailbox are not connected in this synthetic pilot. Full production deletion/privacy compliance remains unapproved.
